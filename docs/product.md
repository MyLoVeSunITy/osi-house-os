# Product

OSI is a House Management Operating System for condominium owners associations.

## First customer and workflow

Start with one association or manager responsible for a residential building in Kazakhstan. Residents create requests. A manager changes the status. Owners inspect budget records, review documents and participate in demo polls.

## Product hypothesis

A visible record of work, decisions and spending may reduce coordination overhead and improve trust. We have not yet measured savings, collection improvements or customer willingness to pay.

## MVP scope

The demo implements local request management, sample financial export, demo voting and SHA-256 snapshots. The role switcher is a presentation aid, not access control. All balances, residents, contractors and proposals are fictional.

## Alternatives and validation

Interview customers about their actual mix of chats, spreadsheets, accounting tools and property-management software. No named competitor comparison or superiority claim has been verified. Ask why they would switch, who has authority to buy, and which existing data must be migrated.

## Pilot metrics

Targets: 10 interviews; 3 pilot buildings; weekly manager use for 4 weeks; 2 pilots choosing paid continuation. These targets are assumptions, not traction. Capture baseline request turnaround before attempting to claim an improvement.
